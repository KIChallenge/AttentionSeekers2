# Austrian health-policy Begutachtung positions (person-masked)

Paragraph-level positions of Austrian institutions on federal draft laws (Ministerialentwürfe) in the
health area, taken from their formal Begutachtungs-Stellungnahmen, plus chat-formatted SFT rows,
steadfastness preference pairs (DPO) and an institution-attribution split. Built for the persona agents
of a multi-agent negotiation simulator (SV KI-Challenge, use case 2).

## Status: draft, do not publish as is

Publication is blocked until the licence questions below are cleared. See "Licence".

## Contents

| Config | Rows | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `sft` | one per position paragraph | `messages` [system role card, user draft context, assistant paragraph], `meta` (statement id, role, date, GP, ME number, provision references, split) |
| `dpo` | one per paragraph with a synthetic counterargument | `prompt` (multi-turn), `chosen` (holds the documented position), `rejected` (concedes) |
| `classifier` | one per usable paragraph | `text` (institution names replaced by `[INSTITUTION]`), `role`, `split` |

Per-role sizes are in `models/data/stats.json` (regenerated on every build).

Splits: `train` = statements dated before 2023-01-01, `heldout` = from 2023-01-01 on (out-of-time).
Backtest: `bt_train` = all statements dated before 2018-09-14; `bt_eval` = statements on the
SV-OG draft (75/ME, XXVI. GP), so a model trained on `bt_train` has not seen the reform it is tested on.

Roles follow the simulator's role ids: ministry_health, social_insurance, medical_chamber,
contract_physicians, elective_physicians, hospitals, nursing, patients, laender, ministry_finance,
chamber_labour, chamber_commerce. Each role aggregates several submitting bodies (for example
medical_chamber = Austrian Medical Chamber plus the Länder chambers).

## Source and processing

1. Parliament open data, filter list 142 (Stellungnahmen to Ministerialentwürfe), GP XXIII to XXVIII,
   institution attribution through a regex alias table, health drafts selected by ministry, topic
   and keywords (see `data/README.md`).
2. PDF to text (pdftotext), removal of letterhead, salutations, signature blocks, page furniture.
3. **Person masking (CON-1/CON-2).** Every sentence that triggers any of: spaCy `de_core_news_lg`
   PER entity (with a false-positive filter for legal German), academic or function title followed
   by a capitalised token, "Surname, Firstname" lists, a first-name list followed by a capitalised
   token, or an "e.h." signature mark, is replaced as a whole by `[...]`.
4. Paragraph selection: 200 to 4000 characters, at least 60 percent letters, at most 25 percent
   masked, no greeting or transmission boilerplate, no scrambled extraction; SFT keeps the up to four
   paragraphs per statement with the most position markers (fordert, lehnt ab, begrüßt, ersucht,
   Bedenken, Mehrkosten, ...). Page-break splits are rejoined.
5. **Second guard on every training row.** The same rule set runs again on every user and assistant
   text that is written; sentences that hit are masked, the result is re-verified, rows that still hit
   are dropped, and a final verification over all output aborts the build if anything survives
   (fail closed).
6. DPO counterarguments, concessions and rebuttals are synthetic (LLM generated from the documented
   paragraph) and pass the same guard. They are marked as synthetic; only the documented paragraph is
   real.

Masking is best effort, not proof: a rare surname with no title, no first name and no NER hit can
survive. No submitter strings with personal names are included; citizens' submissions are excluded.

## Intended use

Fitting and evaluating institution personas (register, typical demands, steadfastness) in a
simulation that is clearly labelled as simulation. Research on institutional stance in Austrian
health policy.

## Out of scope

Representing or quoting real persons; presenting generated text as an institution's actual
position; any automated decision about people.

## Limitations and biases

Coverage is uneven: some roles have hundreds of paragraphs, others almost none (elective physicians:
zero, ministry of health: very few because it drafts rather than comments). Statements are
formal written positions and miss informal negotiation. Long statements dominate unless capped.
The time split mixes regimes (2019/2020 social-insurance reform). Stance labels used in evaluation
are LLM-derived and spot-checked, not gold.
